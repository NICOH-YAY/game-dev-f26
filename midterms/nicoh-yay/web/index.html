VAULTING AMBITION

A pole. A crown. A long way down.

Mouse: look / Hold left mouse: plant & bend / Release: launch
WASD: tiny nudge / Esc: pause (checkpoint in menu)
Without mouse lock: hold right mouse + drag to look
Q/E or left/right arrows: turn / R/F or up/down arrows: pitch
Aim at the glowing reach dot. Look down for your first vault.
